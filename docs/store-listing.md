# Google Play store listing (version 1.1.0)

Ready to paste into Play Console → Grow users → Store presence → Main store listing. English is the default language; add Urdu under "Manage translations" → Urdu (ur).

Rules applied (Google Play metadata policy): title ≤ 30 characters, short description ≤ 80, full description ≤ 4,000; no "free", "best", "#1", "download now" or other promotional or ranking claims in the title, short description, icon, screenshots or feature graphic; no keyword stuffing.

## English

**App name (30):** BMI Calculator: Workout & Diet

Why: "BMI calculator" is the app's biggest search term and the phrase it already ranks for, so it stays first and exact. "Workout" and "Diet" add the two largest new keyword groups (home/gym workout, calorie/diet). Inside the app and in ads, the short brand stays "BMI Health".

**Short description (74):** BMI & weight tracker, home & gym workouts, calorie counter with desi food.

## Growth plan

**Benchmarks to beat** (health & fitness apps, published 2025–26 studies; methods differ, so use ranges): day 1 ≈ 20–35%, day 7 ≈ 7–15%, day 30 ≈ 3–10%. Good apps reach day 1 ≈ 45%+, day 7 ≈ 20%+, day 30 ≈ 10%+. Check Play Console → Statistics → Retained installers.

**Free levers in Play Console:**
- Store listing experiments: A/B test the first screenshot, the short description and the icon (one at a time, 1–2 weeks each).
- Custom store listing for Pakistan, in Urdu.
- Ratings: reply to every review; ratings strongly affect ranking and conversion.
- Fitness has the strongest seasonal swing: refresh screenshots and release an update before January (New Year resolutions) and before summer.

**In-app (next development step):** ask for a rating with Google's in-app review prompt at a happy moment (after the 3rd completed workout or a perfect day), never on first launch.

## Building the graphics and videos

From the demo build (`tool/demo/demo_main.dart`) screenshots and screen recordings:

    python3 tool/promo/make_store_graphics.py SHOTS_DIR build/promo/store   # 8 screenshots + feature graphic
    python3 tool/promo/make_promo.py CLIPS_DIR VO_DIR build/promo            # store, YouTube and vertical videos

Upload `promo_store.mp4` to YouTube (unlisted is fine) and paste its link in the listing's video field. It has no "free" or "download" line. `promo_landscape.mp4` and `promo_vertical.mp4` end with "Free on Google Play" and are for social media only.
